How to read a grade

Each card is one stock. Inside it, every strategy that scored that stock, ranked best first. A grade is one strategy's verdict — the same stock grades differently under different strategies because they ask different questions. Grades are A–F; confidence (0–100) is the share of that strategy's criteria that passed.

AMost or all criteria pass — strong setup for this strategy
BMost pass, one weak — decent, worth watching
CSplit signals — mixed
DMostly fail — weak setup
FNearly all fail, or a blocking rule rejected it

Click a strategy row to see the evidence (why, criterion by criterion), the invalidation (what would prove it wrong), and the audit trail (the exact inputs behind the grade).
